Lock-screen, car and headphone skip buttons go back 15 s and forward 30 s (episodes still change on their own when one ends). After an outside interruption such as a call, Siri, or Maps directions with “Pause Spoken Audio” on, playback resumes 2 s before the interruption.

Export copies the pool as one id per line (e.g. HoP-42, HPI-7, HAP-101, HPC-3). Import accepts the same ids, or a list of episode audio URLs, or the JSON exported from the Apple Podcasts backup script; matched episodes are added to the pool.
